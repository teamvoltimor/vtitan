# Complete System Proposals

Welcome to the **Systems** section of the proposals documentation.

Here you'll find detailed, end-to-end robot system proposals that combine hardware, software, and control strategies into cohesive solutions. Each proposal is designed to address specific goals, constraints, and innovation levels for the WRO Future Engineers 2026 challenge.

---

## What You'll Find Here

- **Full System Architectures:**  
  Each file in this section describes a complete robot system, including hardware platform, sensor suite, software stack, and control approach.

- **Comparative Analysis:**  
  Proposals are ranked and compared based on reuse of existing hardware, cost, innovation, and win probability.

- **Implementation Guides:**  
  Step-by-step recommendations and timelines for building, testing, and iterating on each system.

---

## Quick Navigation

- **01-velocity-edge-ros2.md:**  
  Jetson + ROS2 system (not recommended for your hardware).

- **02-minimalist-racer-classical.md:**

  Innovative ML/ViT-based system (for ML enthusiasts).

- **04-ros2-edge-racer-hybrid.md:**  
  **Recommended!** ROS2 + YOLO on Pi5 + AI HAT+ (best balance of reuse, cost, and innovation).

## How to Use This Section

1. **Read through each proposal** to understand the trade-offs and requirements.
2. **Compare the recommendations** to your team's goals, hardware, and expertise.
3. **Follow the implementation guides** to start building your chosen system.
4. **Refer to the comparison matrix** in the `/comparisons` section for a side-by-side feature breakdown.

---

> **Tip:**  
> For most teams, Proposal 4 (ROS2 Edge Racer) is the best starting point—100% hardware reuse, professional software stack, and high win probability.
