# Comparisons

Welcome to the **Comparisons** section of the proposals documentation.

Here you'll find detailed analyses, decision matrices, and technology comparisons to help guide your choices for hardware, software, and system design. This section is designed to provide objective, side-by-side evaluations of the available options, highlighting strengths, weaknesses, and recommendations based on real-world needs and constraints.

---

## What You'll Find Here

- **Proposal Comparison Matrix:**  
  Comprehensive tables comparing all major system proposals, including cost, performance, risk, and hardware reuse.

- **Technology Choices:**  
  In-depth reviews of sensors, compute platforms, and software stacks.

- **Hardware Analysis:**  
  Evaluation of actual hardware on hand versus proposed alternatives.

- **Configuration Flexibility:**  
  Guides and matrices showing how easily you can adapt or upgrade your system.

---

## How to Use This Section

- **Start with the main comparison matrix** to get an overview of all proposals.
- **Dive into technology choices** to understand the trade-offs between different components.
- **Review hardware analysis** to see how your current equipment stacks up.
- **Consult configuration flexibility** if you anticipate needing to pivot or scale your solution.

Use the sidebar to navigate between comparison documents.

**Tip:**  
If you're unsure which proposal or technology to choose, this section will help you make an informed, data-driven decision.
