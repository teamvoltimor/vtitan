# Proposals

Welcome to the Proposals section of the documentation.

Here you'll find all proposals, comparisons, hardware and software suggestions, and system designs related to the project. Each proposal is organized in its own page or subfolder for clarity and easy navigation.

## What you'll find here

- **Comparisons:** Technology and hardware comparisons to inform design decisions.
- **Hardware Proposals:** Suggestions and guides for hardware components and configurations.
- **Software Proposals:** Strategies and plans for software architecture and simulation.
- **System Designs:** High-level and hybrid system proposals for the project.

Use the sidebar to browse all available proposals and their details.
