# Hardware Proposals

Welcome to the **Hardware** section of the proposals documentation.

Here you'll find all hardware-related proposals, guides, and comparisons for the project. This includes platform options, sensor configurations, motor choices, and calibration guides.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Platform Options:**  
  Explore different hardware platforms, including Raspberry Pi, Jetson, and AI accelerators.

- **Sensor Configurations:**  
  Details on cameras, LiDAR, IMUs, distance sensors, and optional additions.

- **Motor Options:**  
  Comparisons and integration guides for LEGO SPIKE Prime, EV3 motors, and Build HAT.

- **Calibration Guides:**  
  Step-by-step instructions for calibrating hardware components, such as the Hailo AI HAT+.
